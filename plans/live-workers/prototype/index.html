pir — talk to a worker

A run's live view, where you can now pick a task and open its worker's conversation. pir holds a live line to every worker, so what you type, and what pir itself sends, reaches the worker straight away.

Try it: ↑↓ or click to pick a task, → to open its conversation, ← to come back. Inside a conversation, just type and press ↵ to send. y/n answer a permission request. A question set is answered like Claude's own: ↑↓ move, space pick, ↵ next. Tab switches between one line per step and full detail.
