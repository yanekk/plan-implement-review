pir — run control

pir <slug> starts a plan detached. pir on its own opens a dashboard of every run on the machine — pick one to watch its live task block, Esc to step back, Esc again to quit.

Try it: type pir to open the dashboard, or pir release-notes to start a plan. Inside: ↑↓ or click to pick, ↵ to open, Esc to go back. Stop and remove are chorded and double-confirmed like claude agents — Ctrl+S twice to stop, Ctrl+X twice to remove.
